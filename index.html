Rudimentary Pathology

Welcome to Rudimentary Pathology, a reference resource to help pathology residents study and learn pathology! The resource is structured around the topics of The American Board of Pathology's outline for the Primary Certification Exams. Insprired to make something 'like Kurt's notes but with better pictures' by one of my attendings, here is the work-in-progress result.
